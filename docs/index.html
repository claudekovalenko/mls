Setup

Connect Airtable

Airtable holds the searches and the houses. Paste a Personal Access Token with data.records:read and data.records:write on your base, plus the base ID. Both are stored only in this browser and sent only to api.airtable.com.

Where do I get these?

Token: airtable.com/create/tokens → add the two record scopes above, grant access to your base.

Base ID: open the base, the URL starts airtable.com/app… — that app… part is the ID.

Photos

The listing feed carries no photos, and the listing sites block fetching theirs. So every card carries a See it from the street tile that opens the Google Maps curb view — no key, no account, no card on file. For a fixer hunt the kerb view is close to the point, since a tired roof and an overgrown yard show from the road.

Optional: pasting a Google Maps key below swaps that tile for the photo itself, inline on the card. Google requires billing enabled on the project to issue a key — a card on file, even though this volume stays inside the free 5,000/month. Leave it blank to keep everything free. Stored on this device only; it never leaves your browser.
